About These Factsheets

These factsheets were produced by the Orleans County Department of Planning and Development using data from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS). The ACS is an ongoing survey conducted every year that collects detailed social, economic, housing, and demographic information from a sample of households across the country. Unlike the decennial Census which counts everyone every ten years, the ACS produces estimates based on surveys collected over a rolling 5-year period. The 2020–2024 estimates presented here represent the most current data available and are based on surveys collected between January 2020 and December 2024, providing a more statistically reliable picture of small communities than single-year estimates can offer. Each factsheet also compares current figures against the 2015–2019 ACS 5-Year Estimates to show change over time. All estimates are subject to sampling error. For source data and detailed methodology, visit data.census.gov. For questions about these factsheets, contact the Orleans County Department of Planning and Development.
